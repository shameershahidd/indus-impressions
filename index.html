The Brand

Where Craft Meets Conscience

Indus Impressions is a contemporary fashion and textile collection based in Islamabad, Pakistan — blending sustainability, creativity, and cultural storytelling. Our project is built on the theme of the Indus Valley dolphin and soil erosion, highlighting the environmental challenges affecting Pakistan's natural heritage and ecosystem.

Four upcycled leather bags with Swati geometric weaving panels. Two handloom sarees honouring traditional artisan craft. Each piece reflects the deep connection between nature, environmental preservation, and Pakistani identity — promoting responsible design through fashion.

The Bhulan

Fewer Than 2,000
Remain

The Indus River dolphin — known locally as the Bhulan — is one of the world's most endangered freshwater dolphins. Climate change, river pollution, and shrinking habitats have pushed this gentle creature toward extinction. Indus Impressions exists to make sure they are not forgotten.

Artist Statement

"This collection is a love letter to the Indus River — to the Bhulan navigating its shrinking waters, to the weavers whose hands remember what the land once was, to the communities watching a changing climate reshape the world they grew up in. We made these pieces so that none of it would be forgotten."

— Indus Impressions, 2024

A Final Note

The River is Still Speaking

The Indus River dolphin has inhabited these waters for millennia. Climate change, habitat destruction, and human pressure have brought them to the edge. But they are still here — navigating by sound in the murky waters of Sindh, raising calves, holding on. This exhibition is a testimony that we are paying attention.
